# DON'T LET ME GO BROKE

## One sentence
A swipe-based adult survival game where every decision affects
your money, energy and sanity.

## Target player
18–35-year-olds navigating work, rent, friendships, family
pressure and financial survival.

## Core promise
"How long can you survive adulthood without going broke,
burning out or completely losing the plot?"

## Core loop
Receive scenario
→ Make a choice
→ Stats change
→ Consequences accumulate
→ Pay rent
→ Survive another week
→ Eventually collapse

## Starting stats
Money: £1000
Energy: 100
Sanity: 100
Week: 1

## Loss conditions
Money <= -£500
Energy = 0
Sanity = 0

## Win condition
There is no permanent win.
The objective is survival and score improvement.

## Player actions
Swipe left
Swipe right
Use emergency lifeline

## V1 monetisation
Free game.
No forced ads.
One optional rewarded lifeline.
Potential future premium supporter pack.

## V1.0 feature limit
50–75 events.
No multiplayer.
No accounts.
No complex inventory.
